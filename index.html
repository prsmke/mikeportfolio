01 — About

A fresh graduate building real things

I'm Mike Tancontian, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology graduate. My interest sits where programming meets visual design — I like understanding how a system works and how it should look while you use it.

Most of what I've built so far came from coursework and self-directed practice: desktop applications in C# and Java, C++ exercises, PHP and web work with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, plus layout and visual studies in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop and Canva.

I'm at the beginner-to-entry level and I'm honest about that. What I do have is consistency — I keep practising, I keep shipping small finished projects, and I keep learning the parts I don't understand yet. I'm looking for a team where I can grow into a Software Engineer role.
